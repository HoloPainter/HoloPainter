# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

HoloPainter respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information HoloPainter accesses, how that information is used, and how your data is handled when you use the application.

## 1. Information We Collect

HoloPainter does not collect or transmit personal information, usage analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or behavioral data to the developer.

HoloPainter does not require you to create an account with the developer.

The application does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

## 2. Files and Creative Content

HoloPainter allows you to open, create, edit, save, import, and export files such as 3D models, images, textures, project files, and other creative assets.

These files are processed locally on your device.

HoloPainter does not automatically upload your projects, models, textures, images, or other creative content to the developer or to a developer-operated server.

Files are accessed only when necessary to perform actions you request, such as opening a project, importing a model or image, saving a project, or exporting an image.

## 3. Application Settings and Local Data

HoloPainter stores application settings and related user data locally on your device.

This may include information such as:

* Application settings
* Workspace configuration
* Keyboard shortcuts
* Recently used project information
* User-created brush presets
* User-imported assets used by HoloPainter
* Other local application preferences

This information is used only to provide application functionality and preserve your preferences between sessions.

HoloPainter does not transmit this locally stored information to the developer.

## 4. Microsoft Store and HoloPainter Pro

HoloPainter may communicate with Microsoft Store services to provide information about HoloPainter Pro, verify whether you own HoloPainter Pro, and process purchases.

These operations are performed using Microsoft Store services provided by Microsoft.

HoloPainter may request information from Microsoft Store such as:

* Whether a HoloPainter Pro license is associated with the current Microsoft Store account
* Product availability
* Product pricing information
* Purchase status

Payment processing, Microsoft account authentication, billing information, and other transaction-related information are handled by Microsoft.

The HoloPainter developer does not receive or store your Microsoft account password, payment card information, or other payment credentials.

Microsoft's handling of information related to Microsoft Store transactions is governed by Microsoft's own privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Network Access

HoloPainter does not use network access for telemetry, advertising, user profiling, or uploading your creative content to the developer.

Network communication may occur when HoloPainter interacts with Microsoft Store services for product information, license verification, or purchases.

Other operating system components or services used by your device may also access the network independently of HoloPainter.

## 6. Third-Party Services

HoloPainter does not intentionally send personal information or creative content to third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

When Microsoft Store functionality is used, communication with Microsoft services is subject to Microsoft's privacy practices.

Microsoft Privacy Statement:

https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

## 7. Data Sharing

The HoloPainter developer does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Because HoloPainter does not collect your personal information or creative content on developer-operated servers, the developer does not share such information with advertisers or data brokers.

Information processed by Microsoft Store services may be handled by Microsoft in accordance with Microsoft's own policies.

## 8. Data Retention

HoloPainter stores application settings and user-related application data locally on your device for as long as required by the application or until you remove that data.

Project files, exported files, imported assets, and other creative content remain in locations selected or used by you on your device.

The HoloPainter developer does not maintain a server-side copy of this data.

Removing HoloPainter may not remove files you have separately created or saved outside the application's own local data directories.

## 9. Data Security

HoloPainter is designed to process your creative content locally whenever possible.

Because HoloPainter does not operate a developer-hosted cloud service for storing your projects or personal information, your files remain under the security controls of your own device and operating system.

You are responsible for protecting your device, backups, and files using appropriate security measures.

## 10. Children's Privacy

HoloPainter is a general-purpose creative application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

HoloPainter does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated services.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when HoloPainter's functionality, distribution methods, or data-handling practices change.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will include a new "Last updated" date.

If HoloPainter introduces functionality that materially changes how user information is collected, transmitted, stored, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
